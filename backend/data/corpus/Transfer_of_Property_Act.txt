THE TRANSFER OF PROPERTY ACT, 1882

Section 1: Short title, extent and commencement

Section 3: Definitions
In this Act the following words and expressions are used in the following senses, unless a different intention appears from the context:

"Property" includes land, benefits arising out of land, and things attached to the earth or permanently fastened to anything attached to the earth.

"Transfer" means an act by which a living person conveys property, in present or in future, to one or more other living persons, or to himself and one or more other living persons.

Section 5: What may be transferred
All kinds of property may be transferred, except as otherwise provided by this Act or by any other law for the time being in force in India.

Section 6: Restrictions on transfers
Notwithstanding anything contained in section 5, property of the following descriptions cannot be transferred:
(a) An interest in property which the transferor is not competent to dispose of
(b) A future right to money not earned
(c) A mere possibility of a spes

Section 53: Part performance
(a) In the case of a contract for the sale of immovable property, part performance of the contract shall be sufficient to take the case out of the operation of the Statute of Frauds.

Section 54: Transfer to take effect on death of transferor
(a) Where, with intent to transfer to another person any immovable property, a person transfers it to that other person upon the terms that he shall take effect only on the death of the transferor, the transfer is void.
