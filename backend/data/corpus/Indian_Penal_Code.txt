CHAPTER V: GENERAL EXCEPTIONS (Continued)

Section 80: Act of a person who, by reason of intoxication, caused without his knowledge by another, has so little knowledge of the nature of the act, or that he is doing what is illegal, as amounts to prevention of the knowledge of the nature of the act, or that he is doing what is wrong.

CHAPTER XVIA: OF OFFENCES RELATING TO ELECTIONS

Section 171: Definitions
In this Chapter:
(a) "Election" means an election held under the law for the time being in force in India or any part thereof.
(b) "Electoral right" means the right of a person to stand as a candidate, or to vote, at an election held under the law for the time being in force in India.

Section 172: Abolition of qualification relating to religion
No person shall, on the ground of religion, be made ineligible for office under the State.

CHAPTER XXI: OF OFFENCES AGAINST PUBLIC HEALTH, SAFETY, CONVENIENCE, DECORUM, AND MORALS

Section 268: Public nuisance
A person is guilty of a public nuisance who does any act or is guilty of an illegal omission which causes any common injury, danger or loss to the public or to the people in general who dwell or occupy property in the vicinity.

Section 269: Negligent act likely to spread infection of disease dangerous to life
Whoever unlawfully or negligently does any act which he knows to be likely to spread the infection of any disease dangerous to life, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to six months, or with fine which may extend to five hundred rupees, or with both.
