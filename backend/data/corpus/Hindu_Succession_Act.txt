FAMILY LAW: THE HINDU SUCCESSION ACT, 1956

Section 1: Short title, extent and commencement

Section 3: Application
This Act applies to any person who, at the time of death, is a Hindu, Buddhist, Sikh or Jain by religion.

Section 4: Exclusions from the definition of Hindu
Any person who is a convert to Islam, Christianity or Zoroastrianism shall not be deemed to be a Hindu for the purposes of this section.

Section 8: Order of succession in the case of a female Hindu
When a female Hindu dies intestate, leaving any son or daughter (including the children of any pre-deceased son or daughter), her property shall devolve upon such sons and daughters, and the heirs of such sons and daughters, in the same order of succession as is provided in section 6.

Section 15: Right to succession
(1) The property of a Hindu who dies intestate shall be divided equally among his heirs in the order of succession.

Section 23: Meaning of "heir"
(1) Every person who, under the provisions of this Act, is entitled to take or inherit the property of an intestate, is called an "heir".

Section 30: Succession among brothers and sisters
The property of a Hindu who dies intestate shall be divided among his brothers and sisters in equal shares, or if any of them is dead, among their heirs.
