CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT, 2019

Section 1: Short title and commencement
This Act may be cited as the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

Section 2: Definitions
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires:
(a) "Consumer" means any natural person who purchases any goods for a consideration which has been paid or promised or partly paid and partly promised, or under any system of deferred payment.
(b) "Good" means articles which are manufactured or produced and also includes goods which are used by a person before being sold.
(c) "Service" means service of any description which is made available to potential users and includes services in relation to the use of goods on hire or otherwise.

Section 12: District Consumer Protection Authority
The District Consumer Protection Authority shall, inter alia, entertain complaints from consumers relating to unfair trade practices and restrictive trade practices.

Section 21: National Consumer Protection Authority
The Central Government shall, by notification, establish a body to be called the National Consumer Protection Authority for the purposes of this Act.

Right to Information Act (RTI), 2005

Section 1: Short title and commencement
This Act may be called the Right to Information Act, 2005.

Section 2: Definitions
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires:
"Central Information Commission" means the Central Information Commission established under section 12;
"Information" means any material in any form including records, documents, memos, e-mails, opinions, advices, press releases, circulars, orders, logbooks, contracts, reports, papers, samples, models, data material held in any form.

Section 4: Right to information
Subject to the provisions of this Act, all citizens shall have the right to request information from public authorities.

Section 6: Procedure for requesting information
The request for information shall be made in writing and should contain the particulars of the information sought.
