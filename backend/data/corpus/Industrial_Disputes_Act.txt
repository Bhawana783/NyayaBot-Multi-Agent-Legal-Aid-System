INDUSTRIAL DISPUTES ACT, 1947

Section 1: Short title and commencement

Section 2: Definitions
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires:
(a) "Conciliation officer" means a person appointed by the Government
(b) "Dispute" means any dispute or difference between employers and workmen
(c) "Employer" includes any department of Government
(d) "Strike" means a cessation of work by a body of workmen acting in combination or a concerted action with a view to compelling their employer to accede to some demand

Section 9: Reference to Board
(1) Where a dispute exists or is apprehended, the appropriate Government may, at any time, refer the dispute to a Board

Section 10: Reference to Court
(1) Where a dispute exists or is apprehended, the appropriate Government may, at any time, refer the dispute to a Court constituted under this Act

Section 11: Powers and procedure of Conciliation Officer
(1) A Conciliation Officer shall assist the parties to the dispute in arriving at a settlement

Section 15: Constitution of Court
(1) The appropriate Government shall constitute one or more Courts for the investigation and settlement of disputes

Section 25: Conditions for retrenchment
(1) No workman whose period of continuous service is one year or more shall be retrenched unless—
(a) the employer has served on him a notice in writing
(b) the employer has served on him compensation equal to fifteen days' average pay for every year of continuous service
